Privacy

The website does not have a customer account, payment form or website booking database. Car selections and enquiry text stay in your current browser page until you choose to open WhatsApp.

WhatsApp links contain the message you prepare. Opening one shares that text with WhatsApp; you then decide whether to send it to the workshop. Do not include sensitive documents or payment details in an enquiry.

If you tap “Send my location on WhatsApp”, your browser asks for permission to share your location. If you allow it, a map link to your current position is added to the WhatsApp message you then choose whether to send. The website does not store or transmit your location anywhere else. Your language choice (English or Hindi) is remembered only in your own browser.

Google Fonts loads fonts when you visit. Google Maps loads when you choose to load the map. If live reviews are enabled, we request public business reviews from Google; author images may load from Google. These services and the hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs. Their retention practices are governed by their own policies.

See Google’s Privacy Policy and WhatsApp’s Privacy Policy. For information you share directly with the workshop, or to request a correction or deletion, call +91 92131 72194 or +91 81788 96377.
